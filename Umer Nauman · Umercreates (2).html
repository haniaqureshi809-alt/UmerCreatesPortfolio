RUNEBOUND

An MMORPG built from scratch

Runebound is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game that I designed and built myself. It is in beta right now, and I'm polishing it for launch.

Players, stories, and technology all meet in one shared world. That's the idea behind everything I make.

    I made Runebound with the help of Claude. It taught me, debugged with me, and kept me going as a beginner.

    A beginner with a big project

    I'm 14 and I started software with no experience. Instead of waiting until I felt ready, I picked something huge: an MMORPG.

    With Claude's help I learned how games are structured, fixed bugs I didn't understand yet, and turned an idea into Runebound, which now runs in beta.
